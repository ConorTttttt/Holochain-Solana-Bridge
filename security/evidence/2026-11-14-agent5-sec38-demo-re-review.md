**Native RPC failure reproduced in actual validator CI evidence (not reproduced on a separate validator operated by SEC):** `verify-witness-bundle.mjs` failed with `ATTR_INSTRUCTION_ACCOUNTS` for the genuine included failed `wrong admin must fail` transaction signature `3Dkp7uCnsjDfqeHC6seUKBo7Yb3FrhtNXQ7uaKNJZff66MEnCZdpLe3RZf6ND3tM67T9YKfA1uyqv6W4jm2cEWhD`. Its archived instruction-requested admin role was `{signer:true,writable:false}`; decoding the signed compiled-message *effective* privileges yields `{signer:true,writable:true}`. Both had the same account address `7DFxHRbmDmGgzGN9gaDNjh81n72KVSfVgf2D1fMm6e7c`. The logged transaction itself was validly included and rejected by the program as `ConstraintHasOne` (error 2001), so this is not an expected program-execution failure being incorrectly classified.

**Root-cause source trace:** `tests/solana/integration.test.mjs` `submitOnChain()` archives `ix.keys` flags in `record.instructions[*].accounts` and `record.instructionAccounts` **before** `Transaction(...).add(ix).sign(...signers)`. Meanwhile `tests/solana/signed-message.mjs` `decodeSignedWire()` reconstructs **message-header-derived effective** privileges and `assertArchiveAttribution()` compares those directly to both *requested* archival arrays. Compiled Solana messages contain globally elevated union/fee-payer privileges; they do not preserve per-instruction originally requested `isWritable` flags. A fee-payer signer may be effectively writable even where the individual instruction requested readonly. A blanket 'ignore writable' switch would conceal meaningful tamper. Do not label precompile requested flags as provable from the message.

**Required remediation without weakening controls:**
1. Archive `requestedInstructionAccounts` (direct from `ix.keys`) separately from `effectiveCompiledInstructionAccounts` (derived from actual signed message after compilation, or from a separately supplied postcompile source record); distinguish their provenance in schema and labels.
2. Compare effective signer/writable/index/program/data/fee-payer/signature order/blockhash against decoded signed wire AND original `getTransaction`/`getBlock` JSON/base64. Preserve requested flags as producer-provenance assertions with explicitly stated limit: they are not recoverable cryptographically from wire bytes. Never accept unauthenticated requested labels as an independent signature claim.
3. Positive control: original wrong-admin negative execution, an ordinary successful deposit/refund, synthetic signed legacy multisigner and v0 no-ALT, plus *an actual on-validator* negative case exhibiting effective privilege elevation. Explicitly fail if an attacker mutates the signed effective privileges without correct signatures.
4. Rebuild fresh on exact new SOL SHA; require all **three** fresh lifecycle/init-capture/u64-max validator/restart/tamper modes and SHA-pinned artifacts. Root Cargo.lock/Anchor/IDL/ELF/post-sync manifests, public deployment/upgrade authority and installer digests need same-SHA provenance. SEC must reinspect the new source and logs.
