**Demonstrated local-write attacks:** (a) modifying evidence alone is rejected; (b) modifying stored `blocks['10']` and increasing checkpoint to 20 is accepted at recovery, permitting skipped scans; (c) changing an amount and recomputing the adjacent unkeyed SHA fingerprint is accepted. A full-filesystem adversary can also truncate/rewrite or restore an older valid snapshot, alter both journal and checkpoint, seize lock state, and hide replay between independent stores. Checksums/hash chains *without an independently trusted latest digest* do not stop rollback, advancement or malicious rewrite; an HMAC stored with its secret on the same compromised host does not fix full-host compromise.

**Agent 3 threat-aware design + tests:** persist append-only signed or witnessed events and periodically anchor their *monotonic latest root/checkpoint* to an **independent** append-only, access-separated trust domain; bind genesis, program, deployment epoch, canonical tx/occurrence identity and source attestations; use off-host verification/attestor quorum or externally immutable logs, not single-host HMAC as a claimed sovereign proof. On unrecoverable trust divergence, fail closed and rebuild by replay from a trusted independent canonical Solana source; require reconciliation before any economic action. Independent witness/key compromise still needs specified recovery governance.

### Concrete independent checkpoint/witness design: required security properties, not implemented

1. The authoritative append-only external log binds **chain genesis, program address plus immutable deployment/upgrade epoch, SPL mint, Holochain scope, signed transaction hash, outer instruction/CPI occurrence index, canonical receipt PDA, blockhash/parent/slot and all source attestations**. Every monotonic checkpoint binds the **previous anchored root, next sequence number, contiguous processed slot interval, canonical block ancestry and deterministic evidence Merkle root**. A writer may cache snapshots, but may never authoritatively promote its own unanchored root.
2. Use **4 organizationally/key-separated witnesses with 3-of-4 approval** assuming **at most one Byzantine signer**, independently operated from indexer/filesystem/admin/KMS and from one another. Each witness independently verifies completeness, consensus/finality source, immutable scopes, and non-equivocation for every sequence/slot. Two 3-of-4 quorums intersect in at least two witnesses, including an honest one under this assumption; a compromised single key cannot make two contradictory quorum certificates if honest witnesses never double-sign. Quorum signatures are **not themselves independent Solana consensus proof**.
3. Require externally access-separated **append-only/WORM** storage with independent readers and a publicly discoverable/latest signed checkpoint or transparency witness; retain historical inclusion and consistency proofs and fail closed on equivocation. The log must provide a recoverable monotonic latest root **not stored solely with the compromised observer**. Formalize evidence retention, historical ALT/upgrade proofs, fork handling and time-to-finality.
4. On restart compare local snapshot/ledger/event log against an independently fetched latest root; **roll back is rejected**, missing events require contiguous replay from separately verified Solana history, and conflicting roots or compromised signing keys force quarantine/revocation, independent incident review and re-attestation with a new epoch. Globally deduplicate deposit occurrence IDs across every region/indexer with one external uniqueness decision. A compromised quorum or compromised independent chain source remains an explicitly unsolved governance/security failure, not a fallback to economic issuance.

This is a **proposed** security architecture, not a completed OBS implementation, external audit or mitigation claim.
